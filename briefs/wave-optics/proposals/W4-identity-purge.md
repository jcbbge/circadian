## Purge mechanism — why `retire`, not `supersede`

MIND-SPEC's `supersede` means: the loser was an earlier **telling of the same
belief**; its weight transfers to the winner and the lineage is preserved. A
costume fails that test twice:

1. **No genuine self atom subsumes a role brief.** "I am the WS-C worker" is
   not an earlier telling of "I am Circadian" or of "role integrity is
   non-negotiable" — it is the *opposite* claim, made about a different
   apparatus. There is nothing for the weight to transfer *to* that would be
   an honest merge.
2. **Weight transfer would launder exposure.** The 19 costume atoms carry
   ~62 folded weight units earned almost entirely by ack-test flooding (W2's
   audit: 36% of all stack events are ack-class). Superseding them into
   `60b7d685dd27` (the strongest genuine self atom, 7.854) would instantly
   make it a ~70-weight colossus — the contamination's *energy* would survive
   the purge wearing the genuine atom as its new costume.

Therefore: propose a **new ledger event kind `retire`** —

```
{"ev":"retire","atom":"<id>","reason":"<short>","ts":"<iso>"}
```

Fold semantics: weight → 0, status → `retired`; a retired atom is excluded
from render and from subsequent decay; **the file stays** (defocus, never
delete — zoom shows the old telling forever, same as superseded atoms).
Append-only immutability is fully respected: no file is edited, no ledger
line removed.

**Dependency (named, not edited):** `retire` requires (a) a new case in
`foldWeights` and the `LedgerEvent.ev` union in `src/atoms.ts` — **W1 owns
that file**; (b) one line added to MIND-SPEC's "The ledger" event list. Until
W1 lands the event, these lines must NOT be appended (`foldWeights` skips
unknown events silently today, but Law 9 bans silent behavior — an unknown
event must never be load-bearing).
