## What the delta demonstrates

The same ten lines, sealed and unaltered, yield "the mind has no agency"
under one came and "jrg probes with absolute syntax" under the other — the
plate did not change, the basis of measurement did. Extraction is basis
choice: the came's constraint question ("who is the agent?" vs. "what
happened, to which apparatus?") selects which components of the plate's
superposition can appear in the output at all, before any comparison or
weighting runs — the came selects the pane before collapse. Came A cannot
produce an event reading because its question only has identity-shaped
answers, and the plate's loudest identity-shaped sentence is the injected
role brief; Came B cannot produce the costume because its rule assigns
prompt-borne roles to the event stratum by construction. The contamination
documented in `W4-identity-purge.md` is therefore not a bad plate or a lying
quote — every quote is genuine — it is a stacker holding a single fixed came
up to every plate regardless of what kind of light exposed it (the
illumination absence, `docs/OPTICS-ABSENCES.md` §1). Purging costumes cleans
the archive once; choosing the came per exposure class is what stops the
next roll of film from developing the same ghost.
