# Worked example 2 — one occasion, more than one interpretation

**Status:** Paper exploration of an optional augmentation. Every person, event and source below is fictional. The sketches are neither production records nor a chosen schema. Circadian can already retain the narrative; we are examining useful operations around it.

## The experience

Invented source D1:

> Mira hosted dinner. Noor cooked. I arrived late. Mira mentioned Dana, who wasn't there. During the quiet stretch after dessert, I felt unexpectedly at ease. At first I thought it was the familiar company. I'm not sure why it mattered so much.

Later reflection R1:

> Thinking back to that dinner, perhaps what mattered was permission to be quiet. I didn't need to perform or explain myself. I still value the company; I don't think the two explanations exclude each other.

Ordinary Circadian could preserve both accounts, their dates and sources, and recall them together. It need not reduce the occasion to separate preferences. A competent ordinary note saying “later reflection on the same dinner” is our comparison.

The potential augmentation is **making a whole occasion and the development of its meaning deliberately traversable**. A cue about Noor could retrieve the occasion and its later reflection together, while keeping the original atmosphere and uncertainty available. This is a proposed improvement in access and presentation, not a claim that the default cannot remember dinner.

## The smallest supplemental sketch

```text
Occasion: dinner-D1
Narrative: D1
Participants and stated roles:
  Mira — host
  Noor — cook
  narrator — guest, arrived late
Mentioned but absent: Dana

Account I1: narrator's initial interpretation, in D1
  “At first I thought it was the familiar company.”
Account I2: narrator's later interpretation, in R1
  “perhaps what mattered was permission to be quiet.”
Connection: I2 revisits I1 about dinner-D1; explanations may coexist.
Neither account establishes what everybody else felt.
```

These labels organize this example only. No permanent person registry, canonical identity assignment or migration is required by the idea. If two accounts might concern different people or occasions, keep the connection uncertain rather than merge them on a shared name.

The narrative remains the source of atmosphere and meaning. The supplemental part points into it; it does not exhaust the experience. No one is required to turn a meaningful evening into a reusable procedure or a lesson.

## Three concrete uses

**A relevant cue.** “What was that evening with Noor when I felt comfortable being quiet?” The proposed operation follows Noor's evidenced participation to dinner-D1, then follows the explicit later reflection. A short, hypothetical rendering is:

> At Mira's dinner, with Noor cooking, you felt unexpectedly at ease after dessert. You first connected it with familiar company; later you wondered whether permission to be quiet mattered too.

This is a composed recollection, not a verbatim quotation or a claim of proven causality. Its source links can open the original account and reflection. Whether this operation improves on ordinary retrieval remains unknown.

**A question about meaning.** “Has how I understand that evening changed?” The operation presents I1 and I2 in order with their relationship. It does not choose a winning interpretation. Keeping the initial account visible can preserve continuity even when the current interpretation changes.

**An unrelated wake.** No mention of the dinner is required. Retention does not create an obligation to bring it up. Months later it may still matter when explicitly recalled, regardless of how often it helped accomplish a task.

## What must stay distinct

- Dana being mentioned is not Dana attending.
- The narrator feeling at ease is not everybody feeling at ease.
- Another dinner with the same people is another occasion.
- Separate pairwise meetings are not evidence of a joint dinner.
- A later interpretation is not automatically a correction, contradiction or supersession.
- “Perhaps permission to be quiet mattered” is not “quietness caused the feeling” or “the user always prefers quiet.”

These are boundaries for the proposed operation and its interpretation, not assertions that Circadian currently violates them. If the supplemental annotation misreads a source, a valid-looking record can still be wrong.

## How the six proposals might help

| Proposal | Specific operation here | What could remain simple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — Views | Offer a short occasion view or expand into the full accounts, choosing detail from the sources. | One deliberate rendering at recall time. |
| 02 — Compatibility | Establish that the accounts refer to the same occasion and distinguish coexisting interpretations from incompatible claims. | An explicit “revisits, may coexist” note. |
| 03 — Typed relations | Traverse participation and attribution without promoting mention to attendance or feeling to group fact. | A few source-backed role labels. |
| 04 — Multi-input operations | No procedure needs to be extracted from this dinner. | Leave the experience as an experience. |
| 05 — Joint experiences | Retrieve the whole occasion and follow its attributed reinterpretation. | An event reference and one directed connection. |
| 06 — Checked transformations | Check that a controlled structured view retains declared speaker, uncertainty and account references. | A small comparison of explicit fields, if useful at all. |

The [higher-relations dossier](../05-higher-order-relations.md) examines incidence, simplicial structure and links between interpretations. Here an occasion reference preserves jointness; a directed interpretation link preserves the relationship between accounts. Those ordinary structures can implement the proposed operations. This example does not supply a need for simplicial closure, higher composition or a theorem that interpretations are equivalent.

The richer mathematical question remains open: would a useful operation over several interpretations require constraints beyond an ordinary reference? Merely drawing the connection as a higher cell does not answer it. All six research proposals remain available for other examples.

## Fit and cost

If enabled in a future implementation, supplemental relationships could be authored while an episode or later reflection is already being processed, then read during relevant recall. An initial paper comparison uses reviewed annotations; automatic extraction would be a separate capability to evaluate. There is no new startup selector, compulsory person store, database, classifier or retention policy in this sketch.

Disabling the addition leaves the source narratives usable. Links to forgotten or unavailable sources must not reconstruct deleted content or present an old check as current access.

Costs include distinguishing occasions, recording who said what, handling uncertain identities, locating the added references, and preserving the narrative alongside structured fields. A fixed vocabulary may flatten the experience, and an added index may add no benefit where ordinary retrieval already reaches both accounts.

The desired benefit would be appropriate joint recall and preservation of changing meaning with acceptable burden. Counting fact-recall answers alone would miss whether the resulting account still conveys the evening. That qualitative judgment belongs to the person whose memory it is; the agent's fluency is not a substitute.

**The next conceptual decision:** does the explicit connection between an occasion and its later interpretation improve access or understanding over a concise, source-linked narrative note? We can inspect that distinction on paper without choosing a new record hierarchy.
