# Extractor calibration 006

This is a development calibration for all six optional Circadian additions. It is not a held-out benefit study, production integration, or a claim that any model is superior. The working default lifecycle is outside this experiment.

## Authorized scope and question

The operator explicitly selected the existing local Qwen model and cancelled the external comparison after its billing gate. Build the small extraction instrument, obtain independent review, and calibrate locally. No further remote requests, endpoint deployment, billing change, automatic retry, additional model sweep, or production hook is part of this run.

Can a source-reading full LLM produce the six bounded candidate representations, and can a fixed reader consume those representations and actual operation results faithfully? Separate this from the later question of whether an operation improves memory enough to justify its cost.

## Eight preparation bundles

The fixture modules supply one views case, one compatibility case, two typed-relation cases (including an uncertainty trap), one reusable-operation case, one joint-experience case, and two independently extracted old/candidate transformation texts. These are new fictional general-purpose situations. Each transformation package has its own field/reader checks; the pair shares one additional deterministic transformation check. Eight bundles are not eight independent mechanisms or a population estimate.

Fixtures and source-derived gold must be reviewed before inference. Closed fields use exact checks. Free text and evidence entailment require source-grounded semantic review; matching a source quotation does not prove an extracted field. Gold never goes into inference requests. All observed failures remain in the totals.

01, 03 and 05 preparation is blind to the future question. 02 may receive an explicitly task-aware joint formulation and 04 a bounded scheduling task. 06 receives only its own text at preparation time; the two resulting candidates are compared afterward. Caller context carries reviewed vocabulary/state, never answer gold or a hidden future query.

## Calls and fixed configurations

Each bundle uses one structured preparation, one ordinary-prose preparation, one reader of the structured result, and one reader of the prose. The single local configuration has at most **32 study requests**: 16 preparation and 16 reader requests. This supersedes the earlier proposed 64-request paired calibration; the cancelled external configuration receives zero study requests. Extractor and reader remain independently configurable, but both use the same verified local model for this run.

The exact endpoint identities and supported settings are read from discovery/probe evidence, then recorded in per-run configuration and the sealed manifest. No unsupported constrained-decoding capability, weight revision, seed control, or internal reasoning equivalence is assumed. A changed model, setting or prompt requires a new declared run. Tokens, reasoning content if returned, latency and usage omissions are recorded as reported by the service. Local versus remote timing includes serving differences and is not a pure comparison of model reasoning speed.

Transport probes and model-discovery GETs are separately counted. Two model-discovery GETs and three completion probes occurred before the operator cancelled the external comparison: one successful local probe, an external HTTP 400, and one bounded diagnostic returning a billing gate. No further external probes will occur. Peer consultation/review computation is separately recorded and excluded from study-request totals.

No retries or format repair. Any HTTP, transport or served-model-identity failure stops that run immediately. Schema/semantic failures do not cause another sample; the remaining fixed calibration cases may run to diagnose breadth, then the promotion gate is applied. Runs never overwrite or resume a partially completed sample stream.

## Information and operation boundaries

Source texts are inert data. A preparer may propose meanings, evidence and uncertainty, not permission to read arbitrary files, current time, source eligibility, completeness, reviewed identity, applicability or retirement. The caller supplies those fields. Exact-key bounded decoders reject malformed inputs. Missing information is represented only in a form actually supported by the selected operation; missing and false/absent are not interchangeable.

The six adapters call the existing prototype APIs. A report labels its actual checked scope and does not invent an explanation witness. The 06 per-package report is field inspection, not a transformation verdict; the paired check is separately retained. A successful calculation only checks the supplied interpretation.

For this calibration the structured reader sees the decoded candidate and the operation result, while the plain reader sees prose preparation. Both receive the same question and caller context. Neither receives raw full sources separately. Invalid structured preparation follows one fixed fallback: no memory facts, an explicit unavailable marker, and an opportunity to abstain. That fallback is still an extraction failure. This is not the later five-arm ablation and cannot isolate mathematical contribution. It also does not establish context savings, repeated-use gains, or longitudinal coherence.

## Scores, gates and review

Report independently:

- decoding and exact-quote presence;
- required/forbidden/unknown closed-field checks, with critical checks named in fixtures;
- semantic support of extracted meanings and uncertainty;
- actual operation result and declared scope;
- reader required meaning, unsupported additions and uncertainty;
- literal question requirements, with no penalty for unasked incidental detail;
- requests, tokens, observed latency, and separately recorded preparation/reading cost.

Before promotion to extraction development, a configuration must have **8/8 usable structured outputs**, **all critical closed-field checks passing**, **no critical source-fidelity errors** in semantic review, and **all eight structured-reader answers meeting the literal required meanings without unsupported additions**. Plain-reader results are a separately scored comparator, not a hidden exclusion criterion. These deliberately strict easy-case gates diagnose readiness; they are not confidence bounds or a required production accuracy estimate. Noncritical exact-value differences receive review rather than automatic semantic failure.

A different reviewer inspects fixtures, actual operation mappings and rubrics before run freeze. After inference, source-grounded review uses anonymized arm/model IDs, records per-output judgments and identifies disputed cases for independent adjudication. Distinct model calls are not assumed to have independent errors. There is only one sample per bundle/preparation condition; no variance or significance claim is made.

If a gate fails, stop promotion and report the smallest supported diagnosis. Do not expand into the 24-source development or held-out study merely because budget remains. Any later repairs and reruns receive new IDs and remain disclosed development.

## Artifacts and preservation

Only this new research directory is an execution surface. Existing studies and prototype source files stay unchanged. Manifests bind fixture bytes, prompts, runner/adapter code, relevant prototype code, pre-run acceptance and exact configurations. Raw requests/responses and unsuccessful calls are preserved with credential redaction; authorization headers and secrets are never written into research artifacts. Native session history and private memory are not copied into this fictional calibration.

The temporary external credential copy was removed from the private cache when the operator cancelled that comparison. No credential is needed for the local run. The operator controls credential rotation.
