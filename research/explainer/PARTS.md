# Memory examination bench

This is a Figure-sized family: six interchangeable physical mechanisms on one
bench, with two resting configurations apiece. The mechanisms are explanatory
analogies. They do not depict the software architecture or manufacture evidence.
The surrounding page supplies the prototype's real input and result.

The common object is a small precision examination bench. It has a substantial
bevelled plinth, four rectangular feet, replaceable mounting runners, an engraved
scale, mechanical end stops, and seated fasteners. The camera, footprint, materials
and attachment points stay fixed while the tool changes. Only the active subject
has an emerald accent. Words and measured outcomes remain outside the SVG.

## Concept, before geometry

1. **Retained source / aperture.** Three source cards stay in a retaining tray while
   two mask leaves change the reading aperture. Narrow and wide views expose one
   and two cards respectively; the third remains masked by the view cap.
2. **Pairwise / common fit.** Three coded gauge bars share a sliding registration
   fixture. A two-bar match seats; adding the differently cut third bar blocks the
   common cross-key. A local agreement cannot stand in for a common one.
3. **Attribution / sorting jig.** A tagged source card rides one of two adjacent
   grooves. Its speaker pin and subject pin remain physically distinct as the
   selected groove changes. Saying something and being described are different
   relationships.
4. **Three-way / assembly fixture.** Three tongue pieces seat against one assembly
   plate. All three close the fixture; retracting one into its still-supported
   loading cradle leaves the common clasp open. The calendar evidence is required.
5. **Joint event / album.** One bound event page carries three participant tabs.
   The alternative configuration opens three smaller pair pages in the same album
   rack. Separate pairs do not themselves preserve one shared event.
6. **Checked transformation / impression press.** Two comparison slips register
   beneath a checking bridge. Matching impressions allow the seal to close; one
   changed mark leaves the checking bridge raised on its guide shoes.

## Purposeful solid inventory

Every named solid is emitted with `data-part`; flat incised markings, screw dots,
and the soft floor shadow are not counted as solid parts. Exact generated counts
are exported from `instrument.mjs` and checked after construction.

The common bench contributes 30 solids:

- Four rubber foot pads and four rectangular foot stems.
- One lower plinth casting and one inset top cap.
- Four recessed square fastener heads on the cap.
- Two longitudinal mounting runners and their four end stops.
- One front ruler strip and one small blank identification plate.
- Four fixture seating lugs and their four fastener heads.

The aperture tool adds a retaining bed, three source cards, three index tabs, two
back/front guide rails, four rail pedestals, four travel shoes, two aperture mask
leaves, one operating crossbar, two handle blocks, and three tray stops.

The common-fit tool adds a gauge bed, three coded bars (each assembled from two
shoulders and one thin notch floor), six bar retaining cheeks, three tail grips,
three registration blocks, a cross-key with six/eight joined sections, two key
guide blocks, two bridge posts, a bridge, two bridge retaining caps, and a travel
stop. Splitting the key at crossings gives each section its true painter order.

The attribution jig adds a sorting bed, three groove rails, two lane-end stops,
four rail clamps, two loading pads, three source slips, three role tabs, two pin
bases, two keyed attribution markers, a sliding saddle, its active source card,
two saddle shoes, a pull stem, and a rectangular pull grip.

The assembly fixture adds a bed, a central receiving plate, three evidence tongues,
three loading cradles, six guide cheeks, three tail grips, three keyed end blocks,
two clasp posts, a clasp beam, two clasp shoes, and two fastening caps.

The event album adds a tool bed, a cradle, three binding slats, four retaining corners, a
rear fence, three page beds, three paper leaves, six participant tabs, three page
clamps, two binding cheeks, a common clasp and two clasp shoes. In the joint-event
configuration the leaves seat together under one full-width page.

The checking press adds a die bed, two slip beds, two source slips, two rear stops,
four guide pillars, four pillar feet, four guide shoes, two bridge side rails, one
checking bridge, three code marks on each slip, six checking teeth, a seal block
and a seal shoe.

## Construction contract

Geometry is precomputed in the build module with the vendored Anatomy kit. All
parts are rectilinear slabs with real thickness; no round bodies, cables or pipes
are used. Details are closed grooves, edge-rooted scale ticks, face-to-face seams,
and seated dots. Painter order follows sampled slab visibility. Conservative box
intersection and face-contact checks cover each of the twelve resting states;
they are not a round-body or continuous-motion audit. The parent
owns the card frame, crossfade, controls, theme, evidence text and browser review.
