The foundation

The rhythm is already there.

Imagine a notebook that opens at the useful pages, catches observations while you work, writes down the session, and periodically distills what keeps mattering. Circadian already does these jobs.

The six additions are tools for examining what is in that notebook. They preserve distinctions that a loose summary or an unqualified connection can lose.

  1. 01 / START

    Wake

    Bring relevant prior context into the session.

  2. 02 / DURING

    Graze

    Capture observations while the work is happening.

  3. 03 / END

    Sleep

    Draft an episode and preserve the handoff.

  4. 04 / PERIODIC

    REM

    Consolidate episodes and refresh durable memory.

This is the existing lifecycle. The instruments below are isolated research functions; they have not been installed into it.

      What actually changed.
      
    
      
      
      Who
      You set the purpose and constraints. The implementation team built all six. A Fable peer reviewed the code; findings became corrections and regression tests.
      
      
      What
      Six small, independent functions with dedicated fixtures, tests and runnable demonstrations. The diagrams show their behavior, not a new production architecture.
      
      
      Where
      research/prototypes/ holds the code. This explanation lives in research/explainer/. Circadian’s live lifecycle was untouched.
      
      
      When
      The recorded combined validation is October 11, 2026, at 22:45 UTC. These are research-stage additions; their production timing and adapters are not installed.
      
      
      Why
      To make memory usable as qualified evidence, situated relationships and reusable operations—while keeping Circadian’s existing working default intact.
      
      
      How
      Ordinary code implements small mathematical rules over supplied records. No new packages, database or model calls were needed for the prototypes.
