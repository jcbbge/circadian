## The shape of the default, stated as a fact rather than a problem

Three observations about what the working default represents, with pointers:

- **Beliefs are about the agent.** The four atom kinds are identity, doctrine, motif and agreement (`src/atoms.ts:34`, `templates/MIND-SPEC.md:64`). Every atom is a claim the agent holds. There is no record kind whose subject is another person, an occasion, or a place. Others appear only inside episode prose and `USER.md`.
- **The only view axis is project scope.** Wake and explicit recall select by registered slug or by global (`README.md:60-73`, `src/scopes.ts`). There is no way to ask for "everything about Mara" or "last spring."
- **Temporal operations are history-driven.** Decay acts on disuse, pin holds forever, forget is manual, supersede transfers weight and retires the loser (`src/atoms.ts:46-64`, `309-335`). NOW.md is rewritten every session and meal notes are deleted at sleep (`src/graze.ts:19-20`). Nothing represents "valid until" or "applies when."

None of these is a defect. They are the default's shape, and they tell us where an optional record or operation would add an ability rather than restate one.

## Six individual assessments

**01. Coordinated views.** Strongest benefit: recall along an axis other than project, with the view declaring what it left out. Nontechnical case: before a weekend visit, "what do I remember about my sister's family, this year" yields a bounded view with a marker that older material exists. Operation: restrict the population to a chosen axis, then apply the budget, then append an omission marker and source references. Smallest fit: one more selector on the existing recall surface, disposable output, no second store (`research/01-coordinated-memory-views.md:73-85`). What the mathematics contributes: the presheaf restriction law gives one real engineering rule, filter before truncate, and the dossier's own toy example shows the two orders diverge (`01:33-44`). That rule is small but it is a genuine correctness constraint, and the 003 summary that coached rather than remembered is a reminder that a view's job includes preserving qualifiers. Principal uncertainty: whether the view axis exists at all without entities to key it on, which makes 01 dependent on 03/05 for anything beyond dates and scopes.

**02. Contextual compatibility.** Strongest benefit: one continuous memory holding accounts that differ by occasion, person or time without forcing a verdict, and answering "unknown" when alignment is missing. Nontechnical case: "likes lively rooms when dancing" and "prefers quiet when reading" coexist; "preferred late outings last summer" and "prefers early evenings now" register as change, not conflict; two friends' differing memories of one dinner stay as two accounts. Operation: before comparing two records, establish what they overlap on, then report compatible, separate, conflict, or unknown. Smallest fit: an optional applicability line on a record, distinct from write-time scope, plus a report-only comparison (`research/02-contextual-compatibility.md:82-97`). The current COMPARE step receives claim strings only (`02:76`), so this would be new information. What the mathematics contributes: the sheaf discipline that agreement on overlap is a premise, not a product, which is why "unknown" is a first-class answer rather than a failure. The three-way obstruction fixture is a nice demonstration that pairwise checks can miss joint inconsistency, but I do not see a personal-memory case that needs it yet. Principal tradeoff: someone has to supply applicability. If the writer infers it at sleep, 004's lesson applies: the writer imports its own framing into history.

**03. Typed relations.** Strongest benefit: a report is not a trait. "Mira said quiet places help her read, one afternoon in a garden" never silently becomes "Mira dislikes social life." Operation: a record with speaker, subject, occasion and source as separate slots, and a rendering convention that keeps conclusions narrow. Smallest fit: a sibling record kind to the atom, say an attributed report, with those slots and the same verbatim-quote discipline. What the mathematics contributes: the olog path equation is one explicit integrity check, for example recording setting equals occasion setting (`research/03-typed-relations-and-composition.md:57-63`). The dossier is candid that this resembles ordinary joins (`03:69-71`). Principal uncertainty: whether the agent, reading a narrow record, actually keeps the conclusion narrow. The lab showed models broaden anyway, so the value is in what gets stored and surfaced, not in a guarantee about inference.

**04. Multi-input operations.** Strongest benefit: remembering a method with its bindings and remaining work, and retiring it when its conditions end. This is the proposal with the mnemonic in it. Nontechnical case: the afternoon-planning recipe in the dossier (`research/04-multi-input-operations.md:25-47`), or a language-learning hook the agent devised for a confusable verb pair that should disappear once the confusion stops recurring. Operation: a record with input slots, applicability, bound inputs with their source versions, and an explicit residual. Smallest fit: one recipe as data, instantiated by a small deterministic function, no executor, no router (`04:59-71`). What the mathematics contributes: nothing yet beyond function composition, and the dossier says so (`04:21-23`). The partial-evaluation framing is the useful one: retain the remaining work, not the answer. Principal risk: discovery cost. The operator's gateway experience is the right caution. A recipe that must be found before each task may cost more than solving directly.

**05. Joint experiences.** Strongest benefit: an occasion is a thing, not a set of pairwise associations. Nontechnical case: the dinner where Mara hosted, Ivo cooked, the narrator arrived late and felt unexpectedly at ease (`research/05-higher-order-relations.md:29-33`). A later cue about Ivo should reach that dinner, whole, with roles and the narrator's uncertain explanation intact. Operation: an event record with participants, evidenced roles, and a pointer into the narrative. Smallest fit: event incidence only, no closure, no face maps (`05:68-72`). What the mathematics contributes: two things. The hypergraph-versus-clique distinction is a real representational choice: three pairwise meetings and one shared dinner must not collapse to the same triangle (`05:36-42`). And the globular sketch isolates one operation the ledger lacks: a directed relation between two interpretations of the same event where neither is retired. Supersession transfers weight and marks a loser; contradiction opens an undirected tension. "I first thought it was the company; later I thought it was permission to be quiet" is neither. It is reinterpretation with both accounts kept. That is a new ledger-shaped operation, and it is small. Principal uncertainty: identifying people across accounts and distinguishing mention from participation, which the dossier flags (`05:74`).

**06. Checked transformations.** Strongest benefit: a transformation that happens today, supersession, carries an inspectable account of what it preserved. Today a supersede event is winner, loser and timestamp (`src/atoms.ts:46-64`), decided by a single COMPARE token (`templates/MIND-SPEC.md:98-105`). Nontechnical case: "often prefers quiet conversation with close friends after a tiring day" must not be superseded by "dislikes social occasions" without the modality and circumstance change being visible (`research/06-checked-memory-transformations.md:27-47`). Operation: recompute a named property over the actual old and new records, and record pass, fail, or not checked. Smallest fit: a scope-and-modality comparison attached to supersede and resolve events as a reason or receipt, never self-certified by the proposer. What the mathematics contributes: higher inductive types and virtual equipment contribute nothing now, and the dossier agrees (`06:57`, `63`). Proof-carrying code contributes one discipline: the generator does not grade itself (`06:67-69`). The lab's clearest augmentation-relevant lesson supports this proposal's humility: a valid citation accompanied a wrong speaker in 004, so a check may only claim what it checked. Principal uncertainty: whether receipts improve later understanding beyond recomputation, which the dossier already names (`06:97`).

## Interactions I missed before

Reading all six together, they resolve into four conceptual operations rather than six proposals or one.

1. **Others and occasions as first-class.** This is the shared core of 03 and 05, and it is the largest capability gap for general-purpose memory: the default remembers the agent's beliefs and one user; it has no record for a friend, a dinner or a place. Everything else keys on this.
2. **Applicability and retirement.** The shared core of 02 and 04. Applicability says when a memory holds; retirement says when an aid is done. Both are conditions evaluated against the present at wake, not against the memory's usage history. This is where the temporary self-authored aid belongs: a memory the agent writes for itself with an until-condition, which can be a date, an event in the ledger, or a sentence the agent checks at wake.
3. **Views along those axes.** 01, once 1 exists.
4. **Non-destructive relations and receipts.** The reinterprets relation from 05 and the supersession receipt from 06 are both additions to the ledger vocabulary. Neither changes weight. Both make a change in meaning visible instead of silent.

A worked combination: the friend's surgery. The aid "until the third, do not propose the hiking trip; after, ask once how recovery went" needs a person record (1), an until-condition (2), surfaces in a person view before the visit (3), and when it retires, the receipt records that the underlying memory of the friend's surgery was kept while the aid was dropped (4). That is one small lifecycle across four operations, and no part of it needs a solver, a database, or a classifier.

Where temporary aids live in the default today: NOW.md and meal notes, both single-session. The gap is a multi-session aid with a stated end. Pin keeps forever and forget is manual; an aid is a pin with a condition.

## What the laboratory tells us about augmentations, not about the model

Four findings carry over cleanly. Selection has a cost: Jev read the full source on every call (`research/lab/REAL-SESSION-RESULTS.md:84`), so any augmentation that puts a chooser in front of reading must pay for it visibly. Format validity is not meaning: 69 resolving references with a wrong speaker among them (`research/lab/CALIBRATION-RESULTS.md:30`), so a check may claim only its named property. Writers import their framing into history (`CALIBRATION-RESULTS.md:42`), so structured records authored at sleep should point at source spans rather than restate them. And recall quizzes could not see memory quality at all (`REAL-SESSION-RESULTS.md:35-39`), which settles how augmentations should be evaluated: not by questions.

## What an evaluation should measure

The unit of evidence is a wake, not a question. For a small set of authored multi-week situations, personal and technical in separate groups, with a controlled clock, compare default wake against default-plus-augmentation on cloned mind states at several later dates. Judge each wake on three things: did the applicable memory appear, did inapplicable or retired material stay out, and could the agent reach the source in one step. Cost sits beside it: wake tokens added, extra calls, and authoring burden at sleep. Meaningful memory gets its own check: a rare event with no later task value must still be reachable through a person or occasion view after decay would have sunk its atoms. Model and prompts are named and runs are visible. The stopping condition is predeclared: if an augmentation changes wakes for the better in fewer situations than it changes them for the worse, or adds more than a declared token overhead, it stops. None of this is authorized now.

## Prioritization and one bounded next discussion

Most promising: persons and occasions as first-class, then applicability with retirement. Together they are where the general-purpose scope and the mnemonic question meet. Clear but modest: views along those axes, and the reinterprets relation. Least clear: supersession receipts, because recomputation may do the same job. Theory only, for now: nested recipes, the sheaf audit, simplicial closure, higher inductive types.

What would change my view: if, working through the operator's own recent weeks, person and occasion records turn out to be rarely needed because episode prose plus scope already serves recall, then priority 1 drops and views over periods become the lead.

The next discussion I would recommend is a single worked example on paper. Take one invented week containing a shared dinner, a friend's upcoming surgery, a learning hook, and one ordinary project session. Write, by hand, what the mind would contain under the default and under each of the four operations, as the Markdown and ledger lines they would actually be. Then write what wake would say on three later dates. One sitting, no code, no model. It decides which operations earn a record shape and which were only vocabulary, and it gives the operator something concrete to react to before anything is built.
