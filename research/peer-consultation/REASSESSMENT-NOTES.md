# Reading the augmentation reassessment

The [new peer assessment](PEER-AUGMENTATION-REASSESSMENT.md) answers the corrected assignment: optional additions to working Circadian. It substantially changes the peer's earlier judgment. The response is preserved verbatim; these qualifications belong to the parent.

## What changed

The peer withdrew its repair/audit recommendations and its earlier dismissal of four proposals as mere refinements. It now identifies distinct possibilities across all six and suggests four overlapping operations: explicit people/occasion relationships, applicability and retirement, coordinated views, and retained reinterpretations/check receipts. These are proposed ways to think about augmentation, not a chosen plugin architecture.

Its strongest positive judgment is toward explicit person/event relationships and conditional aids. It is less persuaded by durable transformation receipts and the full mathematical machinery. It recommends a single fictional worked example before implementation. No proposal has been discarded and no experiment has resumed.

## Factual and methodological qualifications

- **Existing memory can contain other people and occasions.** Atom kinds do not impose a subject restriction: `claim` is free text in [the atom interface](../../src/atoms.ts). The peer's distinction can support explicit indexing or operations over those relationships; it does not prove Circadian cannot remember them today.
- **Retrieval is broader than project selection alone.** The scoped recall CLI includes `--since`, and [the relational index](../../src/relindex.ts) accepts textual queries with lexical/entity/temporal scoring. The peer's absolute claim that there is no way to ask about a person or period is unsupported. A deliberate person/occasion view could still be an enhancement, but its benefit is a hypothesis about better retrieval or presentation.
- **NOW can carry information forward.** [SLEEP's prompt](../../src/sleep.ts) receives existing NOW. Rewriting it each session does not make its contents necessarily single-session. Explicit conditions may improve reliable use and retirement without introducing a previously inexpressible concept.
- **The proposed path equation concerns the occasion's declared setting.** The [typed-relations dossier](../03-typed-relations-and-composition.md) explicitly separates where an episode was recorded from where its occasion occurred. The peer's phrase equating recording setting with occasion setting reverses that distinction.
- **Wake is one evaluation surface, not the universal unit.** A conditional aid might be assessed across wakes; an explicit recall view, recipe or transformation needs evidence at the point where that operation is used. The existing studies do not show that all questions are useless for evaluation; their particular recall scores missed important differences.

These limits affect the justification and prioritization, not the permission to explore. An augmentation can improve an existing ability without proving it was impossible before. The useful contribution here is a set of candidate operations and examples. Their incremental benefit, integration cost and mathematical contribution remain open questions.
