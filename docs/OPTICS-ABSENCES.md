# OPTICS-ABSENCES — a design register of three missing theories

Registered by wave-optics W4 (`briefs/wave-optics/W4-archive-and-absences.md`),
2026-08-09. This is a register, not a proposal: it names holes in circadian's
memory optics so conventional assumptions cannot fill them silently. Design
authority remains `mind/MIND-SPEC.md`.

## 1. Illumination — no theory of the light a session shines

Circadian has no concept of what kind of light exposes the plate. A session
has a source (user dialogue, worker brief, self-test, another repo's pane),
a class (design, execution, compliance probe), and a coherence (one sustained
line of thought vs. scattered fragments) — and today none of these exist in
the model: every session illuminates identically, so a role brief beamed at a
disposable herdr pane writes the emulsion with the same spectrum as three
hours of the user thinking aloud. **The conventional assumption that fills
this silently:** "all input is the user's voice" — provenance of the light
collapses to a single implicit source, which is exactly how "I am
orch-c001-gate" became this mind's identity (see
`briefs/wave-optics/proposals/W4-identity-purge.md`).

## 2. Exposure — no metering, no reciprocity, no saturation

Circadian has no theory of how much authority a given illumination earns. A
2-turn ACK test and a 3-hour design session deposit with equal authority:
same stack +1, same decay eligibility, same render candidacy. There is no
speed rating on the emulsion, no reciprocity law (long-low vs. short-bright
exposures are indistinguishable), and no saturation point (36% of all 795
stack events came from ack-class episodes, per the coordinator's 2026-08-09
audit — repetition floods without limit). W2 is the first partial
instrument: `briefs/wave-optics/W2-exposure-metering.md` builds a
flash/standard classifier at stack time. **The conventional assumption that
fills this silently:** "frequency equals importance" — count-as-weight, the
default of every naive memory system, which lets the cheapest sessions
outshout the deepest.

## 3. The unexposed plate — no theory of fresh capacity

Circadian only theorizes the written. Weight, decay, supersede, render — all
operate on atoms that exist. There is no model of the writable: what the
population lacks, which strata are thin (34 identity atoms vs. 69 doctrine —
is that health or blindness?), what questions no episode has ever exposed, or
how much unexposed capacity the finite body (Law 4) still holds. A mind that
cannot see its own blank plate cannot direct light toward it. **The
conventional assumption that fills this silently:** "absence of belief is
absence of question" — silence in the population reads as settled rather
than unasked, so gaps compound invisibly instead of becoming targets.

## Conjugate-pair note

Provenance (which-plate attribution: every atom keeps its file, verbatim
quotes, `[ep:]` stamps, resolvable to its episode via git) and cross-plate
interference (recognizing one belief wearing many tellings across episodes)
cannot be maximized in the same measurement. Circadian currently maximizes
provenance — which is why paraphrase flocks accumulate: claims sourced to
different plates are never allowed to superpose. The interference instrument
(`briefs/wave-optics/W3-flock-merge.md`) erases which-plate identity during
comparison only — claims are compared as claims, not as sourced records —
then supersede events preserve full lineage at rest. The pair is conjugate,
not contradictory: attribution at rest, superposition in the instrument.
